Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about booking, payments, refunds, or reports? We are here to help.

Simply select your district from our search bar or district cards, pick your preferred hospital or specialist doctor, select your date & time slot, and click Confirm. You will receive an instant SMS and WhatsApp appointment slip.
We support all major Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit & Credit Cards, Netbanking, as well as a convenient 'Pay-at-Hospital Counter' option.
You can cancel or reschedule any appointment up to 2 hours prior to the OPD slot time from your account or SMS link. 100% of prepaid fees will be refunded automatically to your bank account within 24-48 hours.
Video consultation runs directly inside your web browser or smartphone without requiring high bandwidth. Once booked, a direct video room link is sent on SMS. After the video session, your digital prescription is downloaded in 1-click.
All prescriptions and laboratory test results are archived inside your digital health records dashboard. You will also receive a secure PDF link directly on your registered WhatsApp number.
